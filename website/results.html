Interpretation

These findings answer different questions.

The query-led workflow tests whether retrieved related markets can improve a forecast for a user-supplied target question. The event correction test starts from a known coherent Polymarket event family and asks whether sibling probabilities should be normalised. That is why the final claim is deliberately balanced rather than absolute.
