What the backtests do not claim.

Direct targets are unusually strong.

When an exact market exists, it is already a high-quality signal.

Retrieval evidence is contextual.

Related markets are most useful for explanation and triangulation, not automatic override.

Normalisation is conditional.

It applies to coherent event families with sibling Yes markets, not every market cluster.

Coverage is uneven.
